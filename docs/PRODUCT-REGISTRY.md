## Registration policy

A product is registered only when its responsibility, prohibited responsibility, canonical source, trust domain, and evidence outputs are explicit. Product status is evidence-backed and must distinguish repository implementation, CI, deployment, provider review, publication, installation and useful outcome as separate states.
